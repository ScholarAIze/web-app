# Welcome to Scholaraize

We're excited to launch Scholaraize, a platform dedicated to developing innovative AI applications for scholars across all disciplines.

## Our Mission

Our mission is simple: **bring cutting-edge AI research into the hands of the people who need it most** - the scholars and researchers working to advance human knowledge.

## What We Do

We develop AI-powered tools that:

- Solve real research problems
- Are accessible without technical expertise
- Work offline for data privacy
- Support diverse research domains

## Featured Project: BacTermFinder

Our first major project, [BacTermFinder](/projects/bactermfinder), uses a CNN ensemble to identify transcription terminators in bacterial genomes. Published in NAR Genomics and Bioinformatics, it represents the largest collection of bacterial terminators assembled to date.

## What's Coming

This is just the beginning. We're working on:

- More AI tools for biological research
- User-friendly interfaces for complex models
- Documentation and tutorials
- A blog to share our research and insights
